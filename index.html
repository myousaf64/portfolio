About

Sitting with the people who use the system

At TAQA, from October to November 2026, I was embedded with business teams instead of an engineering team. The people I worked with were not developers. They did know exactly which documents slowed their week down.

So they built the assistants, and I taught them how. We put RAG knowledge assistants over their own SharePoint content with Copilot Studio and Azure AI Foundry, then document-processing and workflow agents with Power Automate and Power Apps. I advised on data ingestion, Azure deployment and Microsoft Teams integration, so the prototypes left the demo and reached daily use.

That is the work I want. A system an engineer admires and nobody opens has failed. The part I am good at is sitting with the people who will use a thing, then handing it over so they can keep changing it without me. I study for a BSc in Software Development and AI Applications at 42 Abu Dhabi, where I also sit on the AI/ML club board.
