  • EMX Network Twin

    Logistics decision platform: an end-to-end digital twin for 3 UAE delivery networks and 22 facilities, combining backend services, optimization and AI-assisted workflows.

    Context
    Private project, August 2026. 3 UAE delivery networks and 22 facilities.
    My role
    Backend services, the optimization and simulation engines, and the user-facing application.
    Problem
    Plan fleet, staffing, zones and facilities across those networks under weekly cost and SLA constraints.
    Approach
    OR-Tools CP-SAT for the allocation. Seeded SimPy and Monte Carlo runs, 50 replications with confidence intervals and baseline comparisons. A human-reviewed LLM interface turns a plain-language request into a structured scenario, and deterministic engines calculate the KPIs.
    Result
    An auditable twin covering capacity, utilization, cost and service coverage, with SQLite-backed audit records behind every run.
    • Python
    • FastAPI
    • OR-Tools
    • SimPy
    • React
    • TypeScript

  • FEDROS OMNI

    Self-improving agentic AI platform with evaluation pipelines and application-level observability.

    Context
    Private project, June 2026.
    My role
    The agent graph, the evaluation pipeline and the observability layer.
    Problem
    Raise agent task success and cut the hallucination rate across 50+ benchmark tasks.
    Approach
    LangChain and LangGraph over local LLMs with FAISS retrieval. Automated evaluation pipelines, real-time multi-agent tracing, Arabic and RTL support, and KPI dashboards built from 1,050+ interactions across 276 customers.
    Result
    35% higher benchmark task success, 28% lower hallucination rate, about 40% lower experiment cost and about 2x faster convergence over 5,000+ iterations on an NVIDIA A100.

    • Local LLMs

  • Employee Identity Recognition

    End-to-end computer vision pipeline for employee identification, tuned for real-world lighting and occlusion.

    Context
    Private project, June 2025. Employee identification from CCTV.
